# 🌱 life 

## why 
Life goes beyond being a simple personal organizer. It is an open-source project that transforms personal and professional growth into a gamified experience, making self-improvement more engaging, dynamic, and rewarding.

Built around privacy, transparency, and user autonomy, Life is designed to operate entirely locally, with no communication with external servers or reliance on an internet connection. Sensitive data, such as journals, schedules, and personal information, is protected using high-level encryption, ensuring it remains under the user's exclusive control.

The goal is to bring gamification, productivity, and privacy together in a single platform, turning self-improvement into a continuous journey without compromising data security or ownership.
